Privacy Policy

Uppies is designed to keep your training information private and on your device.

Information stored on your device

Uppies stores the information you enter—such as profile names, workout history, weights, repetitions, and preferences—locally on your device. A limited summary is shared locally with the Uppies widget through Apple’s app-group storage.

No developer data collection

Uppies does not require an account and does not send your workout information to the developer. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technology, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Notifications and backups

Rest-timer notifications are scheduled on your device after you grant permission. If you export a backup, you choose where that file is saved or shared. Uppies only reads a backup when you explicitly select one to import.

Support and TestFlight feedback

If you email support or voluntarily submit feedback through TestFlight, the developer receives the information you choose to provide and uses it only to respond, investigate problems, and improve Uppies.

Deleting your information

You can delete profiles within Uppies. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from the device, although backup files you exported remain wherever you chose to save them.
