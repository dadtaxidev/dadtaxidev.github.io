Together

Training together

Workout Together runs up to four lifters' sessions on one phone at the same time — everyone on their own program, nobody sharing a spreadsheet.

First, everyone needs a profile

Each lifter is a profile, and each profile keeps its own program: starting weights, units, increments, history, everything. Create one per person in Settings → Profile → Manage Profiles with New Profile — a brand-new profile is offered its own starting-weight setup, so your kid's bench starts at your kid's numbers, not yours.

The full profile story — renaming, exporting, deleting — lives in Using the App.

Starting Workout Together

Two ways in, same place out:

  • The toolbar button. With two or more profiles on the device, the Workout screen's toolbar shows a dedicated two-person button. Tap it to open the Workout Together setup directly.
  • The profile menu. The profile menu in the same toolbar has a Workout Together… entry. (With only one profile on the device it reads Add a Partner… and helps you create the second profile first.)

In the setup sheet, check off who's lifting today — you plus up to three partners, four lifters total — and tap Start.

Switching lifters

Once started, a participant bar appears above the session with everyone's name. Tap a name and that lifter's workout takes the screen — their session, their weights, their reps. The phone changes hands; nothing else does.

Each name also shows that lifter's rest status live: Sam · 1:23 while Sam's rest is counting down, Sam · Ready when it's done. So a glance at the bar tells you who's up next — even while someone else's workout is on screen.

Sharing equipment

Sharing one bar? There's an optional rotation for that: with Rotate lifters automatically switched on, finishing a set that starts your rest timer automatically hands the screen to the next lifter in the rotation — you rack the bar, they take the phone and the plates. Tapping a name in the bar always works too, and simply re-anchors the rotation from whoever you picked.

The setting is off by default — if you'd rather pass the phone yourself, nothing changes. You'll find the toggle in Settings and on the Workout Together setup sheet.

Good to know

Auto-switch doesn't fire after an exercise's final set. No rest timer starts there, and the lifter is looking at their result — the app won't yank the screen away mid-victory-lap.

What stays separate

Everything that matters, which is the whole point:

Per lifterMeaning
WeightsEach profile's prescriptions come from its own history. Your deload never touches your partner's press.
HistorySets log to whichever lifter is on screen, and only to them.
Rest timersEvery participant runs an independent countdown — one lifter's rest ending never cuts another's short.
TotalsVolume and rep totals accrue per profile, exactly as they would solo.

The session summary

When you end Workout Together — the X in the participant bar, or End Workout Together in the profile menu — a summary sheet shows how the session went: one row per lifter with their own volume and reps for the session, and a team total underneath. It's informational — dismiss it and everyone's data is already saved, same as always.

"Trained with" badges

Sessions logged during Workout Together get a small two-person badge in History, with the other lifters' names on the summary line — with Sam. Months from now you'll know which squat day was the one where your kid out-repped you.

Team totals on Progress

With more than one profile on the device, the Progress tab's Overview adds a Team card group: combined all-time volume and reps across every profile on the phone. Individual charts stay individual — this is just the household scoreboard.

One more thing

If a partner's profile is later deleted, their "trained with" badges keep their name. The badge records who was there that day, so the history of a good session survives even if the profile doesn't.
