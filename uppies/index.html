Why Uppies

Three sessions a week. Zero decisions.

🗓️

It plans, you lift

Five movement blocks rotate automatically across a simple three-day week — the proven Greyskull LP program, ported faithfully from the classic spreadsheet.

📈

Your next weight, already decided

Hit your reps and the weight goes up next time. Double the target and it jumps twice as far. Miss, and Uppies deloads you sensibly instead of stalling forever.

✅

Honest logging

Reps stay a draft until you check off the final set — so a mistyped number never quietly changes next week's weights. Fix history any time; everything downstream recalculates.

⏱️

Rest right

Automatic rest timers with separate warm-up and working-set durations, big mid-set-friendly controls, and optional alerts for when the phone is locked.

👨‍👧

Train together

Profiles keep every lifter separate on one phone. Workout Together swaps between two people mid-session — each with their own weights, history, and rest timer.

🧮

Plate math, done

Tap any set for the exact per-side plate loadout — with your own bar weight, not somebody else's gym.

📊

Watch it add up

Volume and rep totals per exercise, per session, and all-time, plus working-weight trends and estimated one-rep-max charts.

🏖️

Life happens

Away for a week or a month? Uppies notices and offers an easing-back reduction scaled to the layoff, so your first session back feels good instead of grim.

🔒

Yours, on your phone

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your training data lives on your device, with one-tap backup export whenever you want it. A home-screen widget shows the next session.
