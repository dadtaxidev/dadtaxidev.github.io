Uppies Support

For help, bug reports, or suggestions, email dadtaxidev@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, what you expected to happen, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful, but remove anything you do not want to share.

Protect your training history

Use Settings → Data → Export full backup… before reinstalling Uppies or moving to a different device. It includes every profile on the device. Store the exported file somewhere you control, such as iCloud Drive.

Privacy

Uppies keeps training data on your device and does not use advertising or analytics. Read the complete Privacy Policy.
